Open-source menu bar app for macOS

Switch your Mac’s sound output with a shortcut.

SoundHop lives in your menu bar and switches speakers, headphones, AirPods and Apple TV with a global shortcut. Set one per device, or keep the defaults. Each shortcut is tied to a device’s permanent identity, so it never lands on the wrong output.
