# Privacy

SoundHop collects no data.

- No analytics
- No telemetry
- No accounts
- No advertising
- No network requests

All settings and remembered devices are stored locally in `UserDefaults` on
your Mac. SoundHop does not transmit any information off the device.

SoundHop reads the list of Core Audio output devices and changes the default
output device when you ask it to. That is the only system state it touches.
