# Privacy

SoundHop collects no data.

- No analytics
- No telemetry
- No accounts
- No advertising
- No network requests

All settings and remembered devices are stored locally in `UserDefaults` on
your Mac. SoundHop does not transmit any information off the device.

SoundHop reads the list of Core Audio output devices and the list of paired
Bluetooth audio devices. It changes the default output device and, when you ask
it to, connects or disconnects a paired Bluetooth device. That is the only
system state it touches.
